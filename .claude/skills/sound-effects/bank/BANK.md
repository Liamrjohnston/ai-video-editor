# Sound effects bank

45 sounds, each picked by ear from hundreds and kept because it sounds clean and premium on a phone. The mix builder only accepts these files, matched by sha256 in `bank.json`.

- **Numbers are permanent IDs.** Gaps are sounds that were cut from the bank.
- **Each sound has one job.** Pick the sound whose job matches what moves. Reusing one sound for unrelated movements is how an edit starts to sound cheap.
